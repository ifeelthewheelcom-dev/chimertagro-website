How We Work

Structured Direct-Origin Export Execution

From your initial inquiry with our UK Trade Desk to Bill of Lading presentation from Lagos, our teams manage every stage with full documentary compliance.

01

Inquiry & Specification

Buyer submits target commodity, grade parameters, quantity (MT), packing format, destination port, and preferred Incoterms.

02

Full Corporate Offer (FCO) & SPA

We issue a formal FCO and Sales & Purchase Agreement (SPA) with guaranteed specifications, loading window, and banking coordinates.

03

Warehouse Screening & Grading

Stock is rotary-screened, moisture-tested, and bagged or bulk-staged in segregated bays at our Ondo and Lagos consolidation warehouses.

04

SGS / Bureau Veritas Inspection

Independent surveyors draw composite samples and supervise weighbridge tally and container sealing prior to port release.

05

Customs & Deep-Sea Loading

CBN Form NXP, NACCIMA Certificate of Origin, and NAQS Phytosanitary certificates are issued alongside loading at Apapa, Tin Can, or Lekki Port.

06

Banking Document Presentation

Full set of Clean On-Board Ocean Bills of Lading and inspection certificates are presented via bank channels for smooth cargo handover.

Trade & Payment Settlement

All international export contracts are executed strictly through formal export banking channels and standard ICC documentary procedures.

Accepted Banking Payment Terms
Payment Instrument Execution Conditions
100% Irrevocable LC at Sight / SBLC Preferred Confirmed Documentary Letter of Credit (UCP 600) payable at sight against shipping documents & SGS certificate.
CAD (Cash Against Documents) Documentary collection routed strictly bank-to-bank; original BL consigned "To Order" released upon payment.
D/P (Documents Against Payment) Executed via international collecting bank under ICC URC 522 rules.
Partial Advance + Balance Against BL Advance deposit against SPA & Form NXP, balance settled upon SGS certificate and draft Bill of Lading.
